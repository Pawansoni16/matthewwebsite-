About Matt

There's no place quite like a summer afternoon in the Pacific Northwest.

I've been fortunate to call this area home for over 20 years and have had the privilege of helping clients say the same thing. My passion for serving my clients goes beyond the standard Realtor® responsibilities — I take pride in teaming up with each one of my clients in a personal way. I'm excited to have the opportunity to walk with you through this exciting process.

Matthew works out of the Windermere Bellevue Commons office as an Associate Broker. He has built a team around him with over 50 years of combined industry experience and is recognized as a top producing broker in Western Washington, specializing in residential real estate throughout Seattle and the surrounding areas — from first-time buyers to seasoned investors.
